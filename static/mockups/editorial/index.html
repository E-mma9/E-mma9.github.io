The promise of a homelab, as it is normally sold, is that you can run a docker-compose and get the same observability stack the professionals run. That promise is half-true: you can run the stack. You will not understand it. This site is a record of what happens when a student tries to learn by ignoring the shortcut.

Inside: a Prometheus + Alertmanager pipeline written by hand and tested by deliberate fault injection; two Bash scripts that harden a fresh Ubuntu box well enough to defend in an interview; and a defence of why three €180 mini-PCs are a better teacher than any cloud-credit voucher.

The author is currently a first-line helpdesk technician at Livio in Enschede, studying HBO ICT at Saxion, and looking for internships in cloud or security starting summer 2026. Letters, briefs and opportunities are received at the address printed in the colophon.
